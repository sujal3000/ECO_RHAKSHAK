# ECO_RHAKSHAK
EcoRakshak is a web-based project developed to help people report problems related to water leakage, water wastage and improper waste disposal in their local areas.
The main aim of this project is to encourage people to protect water resources, maintain cleanliness and keep their surroundings clean. Through this website, users can easily submit complaints about water and waste-related problems. The submitted complaints can be tracked, and their status can be updated.
1. Objectives of the Project
- To reduce water wastage by reporting water leakage and overflowing tanks.
- To help people report garbage overflow and illegal waste dumping.
- To create awareness about water conservation and cleanliness.
- To make the complaint reporting process simple and convenient.
- To allow users to track the status of their complaints.
- To encourage public participation in keeping the environment clean.
